05

Conclusions

  1. Quinta da Barroca doubled the account in its first full week

    It added €163.60 of spend and 588 clicks at a €0.28 average cost per click. The account went from €171.26 to €366.34, up 114%, and from 603 to 1,054 clicks, up 75%.

  2. Village unbranded improved, Village brand went the wrong way

    Unbranded bought 65% more clicks for 49% more money, so it got cheaper per click, €0.55 down to €0.50. Brand did the opposite: 41% more money for 15% fewer clicks, with cost per click jumping from €0.46 to €0.77.

  3. Zero conversions is a tracking gap, not a demand problem

    Both Purchase actions are Primary and Active but read No recent conversions. Bookings for both hotels complete on booking engine pages tagged to third party containers, so Google never sees them. Judge results on Mews direct bookings until a BOA owned container sits on each engine.

  4. Pacing is ahead of baseline on both properties, by design

    Village ran €26.59 a day, about €800 a month against the €600 baseline, which is the deliberate maximise setting running to about 20 October, and it is still under its €75 a day ceiling. Quinta da Barroca ran €23.37 a day against a €55 ceiling, with 66 days left to the 29 October end date.

Needs your decision

Village brand cost per click nearly doubled after the cap went from €1.00 to €1.50 on 23 August. Do you want it pulled back to €1.00, or left at €1.50 while it buys absolute top position?
